About INFOCOP

Privacy shouldn't be a full-time job.

Every sign-up, delivery address and old form leaves a trace. Data brokers collect those traces and resell them — your phone number, your home address, your family members — often without you ever knowing it's for sale.

INFOCOP is built to find that trail and erase it. Our AI scans hundreds of broker and people-search sites, files removal requests on your behalf, and keeps watching so new listings don't quietly reappear.

We built INFOCOP for anyone who wants their information to belong to them again — individuals, families, students protecting their first digital footprint, and businesses responsible for their customers' data.
